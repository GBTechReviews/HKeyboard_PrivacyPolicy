The short version

What we collect

Nothing

Text you type is processed on your device and is never collected, uploaded or shared. HKeyboard (the “App”) has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no automatic crash reporting.

If the keyboard ever closes unexpectedly it writes a diagnostic report (what went wrong, the app version, and your phone model and Android version) to its own private storage on your device. It contains no text you have typed. Nothing is sent anywhere. The report stays on the phone unless you choose to send it yourself from Settings › Setup, and the same screen deletes it.

Voice input

The microphone, only while you dictate

The optional voice typing feature uses your microphone only while you are dictating, after you tap the mic. On phones with a speech recogniser, audio is turned into text on your device. On Huawei phones without Google services, the AppGallery version can use Huawei’s speech service instead: while you dictate, the audio is sent to Huawei’s servers to be turned into text, and is handled under Huawei’s privacy policy. HKeyboard never stores or shares the audio, and keeps no transcripts. If you never use voice typing, the microphone is never used.

Network use

Offline, unless you ask otherwise

Typing, correction, prediction and learning all work entirely offline. HKeyboard never connects to the internet on its own, only when you ask it to, and there are four such moments.

Dictionary downloads. When you tap Download on a language, the App fetches that language’s word list. Nothing about you is sent. It is a plain request for a public file, and if it fails the keyboard simply carries on with its built-in words.

Next-word packs. When you tap Download on a language’s next-word pack, the App fetches that public file from the same place and on the same terms. It is optional; without it the keyboard predicts from what it has learned from you.

Handwriting. The first time you write on the handwriting panel in a given script, the App downloads that script’s recognition model. Recognition itself then runs on your phone. Your ink is never sent anywhere.

Live translation. While the translate bar is open, the text you chose to translate is sent to a translation service so it can be translated. Nothing else is ever sent: not the words you type, not your clipboard, not your learned words, and no identifier of you or your phone.

On device

What is stored on your phone

All of this lives in the App’s private, sandboxed storage, which other apps cannot read. None of it leaves your device.

  • Your settings, theme, emoji and layout choices, and notification preferences.
  • The words and next-word patterns HKeyboard learns from your typing, to improve suggestions.
  • Your clipboard history, if you use the clipboard feature (kept on device, and you can pin or clear items).
  • A crash report, if the keyboard ever stops unexpectedly. It holds what went wrong, the App version and your Android version, and no text you have typed. Nothing is uploaded: it sits on your phone until you either delete it or choose to send it yourself from Settings › Setup, using whichever app you pick.

None of it is backed up to any cloud. The App switches cloud backup off, so your learned words and clipboard history are never copied to a backup account, which also means uninstalling really does remove them, with no copy left behind. Android’s direct phone-to-phone transfer is left on, so if you move to a new device your keyboard can come with you without anything passing through a third party.

You can remove all of it by uninstalling the App, clearing its storage in system Settings, or using the App’s own Reset, which can clear settings, learned words and clipboard history.

Never

What we do not do

  • We do not read, view or analyse what you type.
  • We collect no personal data, use no analytics, and do not track you.
  • We display no advertisements and include no third-party ad or tracking code.
  • We do not share, sell or transmit your data to any third party.
  • We do not knowingly collect information from children.
Permissions

What the App asks for

  • Vibrate (android.permission.VIBRATE), optional haptic feedback on key presses.
  • Internet (android.permission.INTERNET), only for the dictionary downloads and live translation described above, both of which you start.
  • Microphone (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO), only for optional voice typing, used solely while you dictate. On the AppGallery version, when the phone has no speech recogniser of its own, dictated audio goes to Huawei’s speech service while you dictate (see Voice input above).

HKeyboard does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, photos, storage or call logs.

Control

Removing your data

Because HKeyboard has no account and stores everything locally, there is nothing to delete from a server. Uninstalling the App, or clearing its storage from your phone’s Settings, removes everything stored on your device. The in-app Reset can clear settings, learned words and clipboard history without uninstalling.

Scope

Children’s privacy

HKeyboard is not directed at children under 13, or the relevant minimum age in their country or region, and does not knowingly collect information from them.

我们收集什么

什么都不收集

您输入的文字在您的设备上处理，绝不被收集、上传或共享。HKeyboard（下称"应用"）没有用户账户、没有统计分析、没有广告，也没有自动崩溃上报。

若键盘曾意外关闭，它会将一份诊断报告（出错信息、应用版本，以及您的手机型号和 Android 版本）写入其位于您设备上的私有存储。其中不含您输入过的任何文字。任何内容都不会被发送到任何地方。除非您在"设置 › Setup"中自行选择发送，否则该报告只保留在手机上；同一界面也可将其删除。

语音输入

麦克风，仅在您口述时使用

可选的语音输入功能仅在您口述期间使用麦克风，且需先点按麦克风按钮。在自带语音识别的手机上，音频会在您的设备上转写为文字。在没有 Google 服务的华为手机上，AppGallery 版本会改用华为的语音服务：在您口述时，音频会被发送到华为的服务器以转写为文字，并按华为的隐私政策处理。HKeyboard 绝不存储或共享该音频，也不保留任何转写文本。若您从不使用语音输入，麦克风便绝不会被使用。

网络使用

离线，除非您主动要求

输入、更正、联想与学习全部完全离线工作。HKeyboard 绝不会自行联网，只有在您主动要求时才会，且仅有以下四种情形。

词库下载。当您对某种语言点按"下载"时，应用会获取该语言的词表。不会发送任何关于您的信息。这只是一次对公开文件的普通请求；若失败，键盘会继续使用其内置词库。

联想词包。当您对某种语言的联想词包点按"下载"时，应用会以相同方式、从相同位置获取该公开文件。此项为可选；没有它，键盘会根据它从您那里学到的内容进行联想。

手写。当您首次在手写面板上书写某种文字时，应用会下载该文字的识别模型。识别本身随后在您的手机上运行。您的笔迹绝不会被发送到任何地方。

即时翻译。当翻译栏打开时，您选择翻译的文字会被发送到翻译服务以进行翻译。除此之外绝不发送任何其他内容：不发送您输入的文字、不发送剪贴板、不发送学习到的词语，也不发送任何关于您或您手机的标识。

本机存储

您的手机上保存了什么

以下内容全部保存在应用的私有沙盒存储中，其他应用无法读取。这些内容都不会离开您的设备。

  • 您的设置、主题、表情与布局选择，以及通知偏好。
  • HKeyboard 从您的输入中学习到的词语与联想模式，用于改进建议。
  • 您的剪贴板历史（如果您使用剪贴板功能，内容保存在设备上，您可以固定或清除条目）。
  • 一份崩溃报告（如果键盘曾意外停止）。它包含出错信息、应用版本和您的 Android 版本，不含您输入过的任何文字。任何内容都不会上传：它只保留在您的手机上，直到您将其删除，或在"设置 › Setup"中用您选择的应用自行发送。

这些内容都不会备份到任何云端。应用会关闭云备份，因此您学习到的词语和剪贴板历史绝不会被复制到备份账户，这也意味着卸载会真正将它们移除，不留任何副本。Android 的设备到设备直接传输保持开启，因此当您更换新设备时，键盘可以随您迁移，而无需任何内容经过第三方。

您可以通过卸载应用、在系统设置中清除其存储，或使用应用自带的"重置"（可清除设置、学习到的词语和剪贴板历史）来移除全部内容。

您的掌控
