At a glance

The short version

  • We do not collect, transmit or sell your personal data.
  • Everything you type stays on your device.
  • The app contains no analytics, telemetry, advertising or tracking libraries.
  • The only network activity is optional and started by you. Both cases are described below.
On device

What HKeyboard keeps, and where

These live in the app's private storage on your phone. They are not uploaded anywhere and cannot be read by other apps.

Personal dictionary. To improve prediction and autocorrect, HKeyboard learns words and word pairs you type. This happens entirely on your device. Clear it at any time from Settings.

Clipboard history. If you turn on the clipboard panel, recently copied items are saved so you can paste them again. Stored locally, deletable individually, and clearable in full from Settings. Leave the feature off and nothing is kept.

Snippets. HKeyboard can offer to save text you type often, such as an email address or phone number. It is saved only when you accept the offer — never automatically. Stored locally, deletable at any time.

Settings. Your theme, languages, layout and behaviour choices.

Never

What HKeyboard does not do

  • It does not record or transmit your keystrokes.
  • It does not learn from or store text in password fields, and offers no autocorrect or predictions in them.
  • It does not save payment card numbers, one-time codes or similar sensitive input as snippets.
  • It does not upload your personal dictionary, clipboard or snippets.
  • It does not read your contacts, photos, files, location or call history.
  • It contains no analytics or crash-reporting services.
Mode

Incognito mode

With incognito mode on, nothing you type is added to the personal dictionary or clipboard history. Prediction and autocorrect keep working from the existing dictionary. Turning it on does not delete anything already saved.

Network

When the app uses the internet

HKeyboard works fully offline. There are exactly two exceptions, and both are optional.

Downloadable dictionaries. If you choose to download an expanded dictionary for a language, HKeyboard fetches a file from one fixed address on GitHub (github.com/GBTechReviews/harmonikeyboard-dicts). It is a plain download. Nothing about you, your device or your typing is sent with it, and a download never starts on its own — only when you tap to start it. GitHub, as host, may log the request the way any web server does; that is outside our control and we never see those logs.

Handwriting recognition models. If you use handwriting input, the app may need a recognition model. That download is handled by Google ML Kit from Google's servers. Once the model is on your phone, recognition runs on your phone — your handwriting is not sent anywhere. The download itself is covered by Google's privacy policy.

If you use neither feature, HKeyboard makes no network connections at all.

Microphone

Voice input

Using voice input requires microphone access. Your speech is converted to text for that purpose alone. HKeyboard does not record, store or transmit your audio, and keeps no transcripts. The microphone is used only while voice input is active, and you can refuse or revoke the permission at any time in your phone's settings — the rest of the keyboard carries on working without it.

Permissions

What the app asks for

  • Internet — only for the optional downloads above.
  • Microphone — only for voice input, only while it is active.
  • Vibrate — for key-press haptics, if you enable them.
Control

Your rights

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to disclose, correct or delete on your behalf. Everything the app creates sits on your device and is yours to remove: clear it from Settings, or uninstall the app to take all of it with you.

Scope

Children

HKeyboard is not directed at children, and we knowingly collect data from no one — children included.

本机存储

HKeyboard 保存了什么，存在哪里

以下内容保存在应用位于您手机上的私有存储空间内，不会上传到任何地方，其他应用也无法读取。

个人词库。为改进联想输入与自动更正，HKeyboard 会学习您输入的词语与词组搭配。该过程完全在您的设备上进行，可随时在“设置”中清除。

剪贴板历史。若您开启剪贴板面板，最近复制的内容会被保存，方便再次粘贴。内容保存在本地，可单条删除，也可在“设置”中一次性清空。不开启该功能，则不保存任何记录。

快捷短语。HKeyboard 可以询问您是否保存常用文本，例如邮箱地址或电话号码。只有在您接受提示后才会保存——绝无自动保存。内容保存在本地，可随时删除。

设置项。您选择的主题、语言、布局与操作偏好。

绝不进行

HKeyboard 不会做的事

  • 不会记录或传输您的按键内容。
  • 不会在密码输入框中学习或保存文本，也不在其中提供自动更正或联想输入。
  • 不会将银行卡号、一次性验证码等敏感输入保存为快捷短语。
  • 不会上传您的个人词库、剪贴板内容或快捷短语。
  • 不会读取您的通讯录、照片、文件、位置或通话记录。
  • 不含任何统计分析或崩溃上报服务。
模式

无痕模式

开启无痕模式后，您输入的任何内容都不会被加入个人词库或剪贴板历史。联想输入与自动更正仍会基于现有词库正常工作。开启该模式不会删除此前已保存的数据。

网络

应用在何时使用网络

HKeyboard 可完全离线使用。仅有两种例外情形，且均为可选。

下载词库。若您选择为某种语言下载扩展词库，HKeyboard 会从 GitHub 上的一个固定地址（github.com/GBTechReviews/harmonikeyboard-dicts）获取词库文件。这只是一次普通下载，不会随请求发送任何关于您本人、您的设备或您输入内容的信息；下载绝不会自行开始，只在您点击后进行。作为托管方，GitHub 可能像任何网站服务器一样记录该请求；这不在我们的控制范围内，我们也从不接触这些日志。

手写识别模型。若您使用手写输入，应用可能需要下载识别模型。该下载由 Google ML Kit 从 Google 服务器完成。模型下载到手机后，识别全程在本机进行——您的手写内容不会被发送到任何地方。下载本身适用 Google 隐私政策。

若这两项功能您都不使用，HKeyboard 不会建立任何网络连接。

麦克风

语音输入

使用语音输入需要麦克风权限。您的语音仅用于转写为文字这一目的。HKeyboard 不会录制、保存或传输您的音频，也不保留转写文本。麦克风仅在语音输入激活期间使用，您可随时在手机设置中拒绝或撤销该权限——键盘的其余功能不受影响，照常使用。
