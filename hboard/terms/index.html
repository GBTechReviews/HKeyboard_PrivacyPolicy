These terms cover HBoard for HarmonyOS. By choosing to use HBoard, you agree to these terms. You may stop using the app and uninstall it at any time. HBoard is an independent project and is not affiliated with Google or Samsung.

Offline keyboard and optional dictionaries

Typing, spelling correction, predictions, glide recognition and local learning run on your device. Optional internet features are dictionary checking and downloading, started when you tap Check dictionary updates for a language. Emoji Kitchen and online sticker suggestions are disabled and hidden in this release; ordinary emoji and emoticons remain available. Your messages and screenshots are not uploaded. There are no automatic background dictionary checks. Fast offline translation is included with the application and prepared in local storage on first use. No separate translation download or file import is required. Supported languages are English, Simplified Chinese, Italian, French, German, Spanish and Portuguese. Translation drafts stay on the device. Translations are generated by an on-device neural model and may contain errors; review them before use. Previously imported Quality and legacy model copies in app storage are retired after Fast is ready. External websites, email apps, operating-system services and the apps receiving your text have their own terms and network behaviour.

Dictionary downloads and licences

Downloads come from GBTechReviews/harmonikeyboard-dicts on GitHub. Availability and language coverage vary. HBoard checks supported licence records, integrity and format before using a pack. Downloaded vocabulary supplements the included dictionary; your personal words remain separate. The source licences and attribution shown in Dictionary credits apply to downloaded data. The repository’s code licence does not grant a new licence to every dictionary. Bundled third-party code, models, fonts, icons and language data retain their own notices and licences.

Screenshots and images

Screenshot thumbnails and access are mediated by HarmonyOS; suggestions are hidden in Incognito and identified protected fields. Selecting a screenshot replaces the system clipboard and requests image paste; the receiving app must support that operation. HBoard does not send a message automatically.

You are responsible for the text you choose to enter, export or send to another app. Please see the Privacy Policy for details of local storage, optional GitHub dictionary requests and your controls.

Backup and review controls

You choose whether to export or restore a local backup. Settings backups and separate clipboard exports can contain personal information; HBoard does not encrypt those files. Keep exported files secure and review the restore preview before confirming. Proofreading suggestions run locally and change your text only when you tap Apply. Review proposed changes before accepting them; Undo depends on the receiving editor retaining the verified text and session. GIF tools are disabled in this version.
