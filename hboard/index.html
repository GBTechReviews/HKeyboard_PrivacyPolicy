HBoard Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 16 November 2026. Applies to HBoard for HarmonyOS, including release 0.1.131.

Publisher and data controller: Griffith Rhys Bowen, trading as HProductions.
Privacy and support: huaweiproreview@gmail.com.

Typing, spelling correction, predictions, glide recognition, voice typing and Fast translation run on your device. HBoard has no remote typing-history account, advertising SDK or analytics service. Optional dictionary downloads contact GitHub; those requests do not include your messages or surrounding text. Emoji Kitchen is disabled in this release.

Typing and the receiving app

HBoard uses the text, selection and surrounding context that HarmonyOS and the receiving application make available to provide typing, corrections, predictions and editing. HBoard does not send that text to a remote correction or message-analysis service. Your receiving app gets the text or image you choose to enter and handles it under its own policy.

HBoard follows the platform's field restrictions. Identified password and one-time-code fields suppress relevant predictions, learning and clipboard features. Some apps provide restricted or incomplete field information, so HBoard cannot identify every sensitive situation or read every editor. It does not bypass restricted text access.

Personalisation and local records

When the corresponding controls are enabled, HBoard can keep personal words, word-choice and contextual counts, correction preferences, touch/glide adaptation and emoji-choice counts in its application storage. An explicit suggestion choice can teach a spelling. Undoing a correction does not add the restored misspelling to your vocabulary. Optional Undo feedback can remember rejected correction pairs locally; that feedback is off by default.

Word learning and Adaptive touch have separate controls. Emoji learning has its own setting. Incognito pauses new learning and hides screenshot suggestions. Turning a control off pauses its future use; it does not erase previously saved data. Explicitly downloading a dictionary in Incognito can still make the external requests described below. Incognito is not a network-anonymity service.

The optional Glide practice & calibration page records the displayed practice word, finger coordinates and keyboard geometry only after Start recording. Recording stops when you stop or leave the page. These recordings stay local and can be removed with Delete recordings. Normal glide input does not create a practice recording of your messages.

App colours and preferences

HarmonyOS can provide the receiving app's identifier so HBoard can handle editor compatibility and choose an app-related colour. When app colour matching is enabled and Incognito is off, HBoard can retain up to 40 app identifiers for its colour chooser. Per-app colours you explicitly set are also stored locally. HBoard does not scan your installed-app list, read those apps' contents or upload these identifiers. Colour matching uses built-in colour information and your choices.

Turning colour matching off stops adding identifiers to that chooser. Previously saved identifiers remain in the bounded list until replaced or HBoard's application data is erased. Per-app colour choices can be removed individually in the colour settings.

Clipboard, shortcuts and saved addresses

Clipboard previews and history read plain text only when the relevant settings and HarmonyOS authorisation allow it. Incognito and identified protected fields suppress clipboard collection. Without clipboard-read authorisation, a system Paste button can grant access for the paste action you request. Clipboard rules try to exclude codes, keys, payment-card numbers and other recognised sensitive items, but cannot recognise every sensitive text.

Unpinned clips normally stay in memory for up to one hour. Longer retention choices can save them on the device for 24 hours, seven days, 30 days or until deletion. Pinned clips stay until removed or displaced by the pin limit. Expired and excess items are removed when HBoard checks its history; the list is bounded to 30 items, including up to 10 pins. HBoard's history controls do not erase the separate system clipboard or copies in another app.

Text shortcuts, snippets saved as clipboard items, custom dictionary entries and optionally saved email addresses remain in local application storage. HBoard does not read your address book or upload these records.

Voice typing, OCR and translation

Voice typing uses the microphone only after you activate it and HarmonyOS grants access. Bundled Whisper models recognise supported languages on the device. Chinese dictation can use HarmonyOS's offline speech service. HBoard uses temporary audio buffers for recognition, does not keep a voice-recording history and does not send audio or recognised text to an HBoard speech server. Stop dictation or revoke microphone permission to stop this feature.

Scan text uses the system camera or photo picker to access the image you select. OCR runs on the device. Recognised text remains in that tool until you choose Copy or leave it; HBoard does not upload the image for OCR. The selected original image remains under the control of the system photo library or its source app.

Fast offline translation is included in the application download. On first use, HBoard prepares the bundled model in local keyboard storage; no separate model download or file import is required. It supports English, Simplified Chinese, Italian, French, German, Spanish and Portuguese. Text is processed locally and translation drafts are not sent to a translation server. The installed application contains the model, and preparation creates a working copy in private storage, retained until app data is erased or the app is uninstalled. After Fast is ready, HBoard removes its old app-private Quality and legacy model copies. Original files that users saved outside app storage remain under their control.

Retired sticker feature

Emoji Kitchen and online sticker suggestions are disabled and hidden. This release does not request Google sticker images, and an older saved preference cannot enable those requests. Ordinary emoji and emoticons remain available offline. Older versions may have stored sticker caches, favourites and recent-combination lists in private app storage. This release does not display or use those records. HarmonyOS can evict cached images; clearing app data or uninstalling removes app-private records, subject to device backups.

Screenshot suggestions and image paste

When Screenshot suggestions is on, HBoard checks the identifier and timestamp of a recent screenshot while an eligible keyboard field is visible. Suggestions are hidden in Incognito and in identified protected, numeric and email fields, and are suppressed while relevant text composition is active. HarmonyOS's RecentPhotoComponent displays the thumbnail and grants HBoard access to the selected image only when you tap it. HBoard does not request broad photo-library access or upload screenshots.

A screenshot offer lasts at most two minutes, can be dismissed, and can be disabled in Settings → Clipboard. Dismissing an offer does not delete the screenshot from your photo library. Selecting a screenshot copies that image to the system clipboard, replacing the previous clipboard item, and requests paste into the same verified editor. The receiving app must support image paste; you may need its Paste control. HBoard does not send a message automatically or add the image to its plain-text clipboard history.

Optional dictionary downloads

Ordinary typing and bundled dictionaries work offline. Opening Languages does not start a dictionary network check. Tapping Dictionary Download requests metadata, licence records and vocabulary from GitHub's API and raw-file services for GBTechReviews/harmonikeyboard-dicts. GitHub receives your IP address, requested language-file paths and ordinary request information. HBoard does not send messages, personal words, touch data or learned history. There are no automatic background dictionary checks.

Downloaded vocabulary is checked for supported licences, integrity and format, stored locally and used offline. Dictionary credits show its source notices. Restore bundled removes downloaded vocabulary without deleting personal words. GitHub handles its services under the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Backups, exports and message review

Settings backup exports the preferences, themes, keyboard and translation language choices, custom words, shortcuts, blocked words and explicit per-app colour choices included in your settings. It excludes the app-colour chooser's observed-app list, clipboard history, the separately stored saved-email list, learned typing history, voice recordings, dictionaries and model files. Restore shows a preview and requires confirmation before replacing the included settings; clipboard and learned typing history are retained.

Clipboard settings have a separate export for saved clipboard items. Depending on the retention settings and available saved items, this can contain pinned text and saved recent clips. Both kinds of backup are ordinary files and are not encrypted by HBoard. The system file picker lets you choose their destination, which can be local or cloud storage managed by another service. HBoard cannot erase exported copies or copies you share.

Proofread reads a complete readable message or a selection made with HBoard's editing controls only when you open it. It uses local dictionaries and rules, previews proposed changes and requires Apply. Review and Undo data remain in memory for the editor session; they are not exported as settings, used to teach words or uploaded. Protected and restricted fields are excluded.

Permissions, recipients and international processing

Microphone permission supports activated voice typing; authorised clipboard access supports previews, history and paste; Internet access supports requested dictionary checks and downloads. Optional device-gesture awareness supports automatic hand detection for layout adjustment, and vibration permission supports key/control feedback. Camera and photo selection use system pickers. HBoard does not request contacts or location access. Some features also require HarmonyOS Full experience mode.

GitHub operates internationally, so information sent in optional dictionary requests may be processed outside your country. Its privacy statement explains its locations, retention and transfer arrangements. HBoard does not operate a remote keyboard-text database or an overseas typing-history service. Opening a policy, repository or email link uses the browser or email service you choose. HarmonyOS, its photo/camera/speech services, device backups and destination apps operate under their own settings and policies.

Why information is used and your choices

Where UK or EU data-protection law applies, processing needed to provide the keyboard features you request is used to perform our contract with you. Local personalisation and support handling also serve our legitimate interests in useful, reliable input and responding to requests, with the local-storage limits and controls described here. Optional microphone, image and online features follow your choices and platform authorisations; you can stop using them or withdraw access at any time. Refusing optional access leaves ordinary typing available, subject to platform restrictions.

HBoard does not make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about you. Its automated word and emoji ranking helps you choose input; you remain responsible for reviewing text before sending it.

Retention, deletion and support

Preferences, custom words, shortcuts, saved addresses, adaptation and permitted local histories remain until their limits expire, you remove/reset them, or HBoard's application data is erased. Turning a setting off is not a deletion request. On-device processing buffers, drafts and review data are discarded as their session or tool ends; the receiving app, original photo, clipboard or exported file may retain its own copy.

  • Use Dictionary Forget/Delete learned words, Corrections and suggestions reset controls, Reset touch learning and Reset emoji suggestions for their respective local records.
  • Delete shortcuts, saved email addresses and clipboard items in their corresponding settings. Delete practice recordings on the calibration page. Retired sticker records can be removed using HarmonyOS app-data controls.
  • HarmonyOS app-storage controls can clear app data; uninstalling removes application storage subject to backups or copies separately managed by you or the system. These actions do not erase other apps' data.

Operational logs can contain timings, feature status and error codes. HBoard does not automatically upload logs or intentionally include message, clipboard or audio contents in those diagnostics. If you choose to send logs or samples to support, review them first.

If you email support, the publisher receives your email address and the information you include and uses them to answer and manage your request. Correspondence is retained while needed to resolve it and related follow-up or legal obligations, then deleted when no longer needed. Please do not send passwords or sensitive typing samples. There is no remote copy of your device-only keyboard records for the publisher to retrieve.

Your rights and right to object

Where applicable, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of personal information held by the publisher. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests by contacting the address above. Local data can be managed directly using HBoard and HarmonyOS controls; we cannot remotely access or erase your device. You may complain to your local data-protection authority, including the UK Information Commissioner's Office.
