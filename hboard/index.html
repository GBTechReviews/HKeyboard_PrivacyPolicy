HBoard Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 12 November 2026

Publisher: Griffith Rhys Bowen (HProductions).
Privacy and support: huaweiproreview@gmail.com

HBoard is a keyboard for HarmonyOS. Spelling correction, predictions and glide recognition run on your device. HBoard does not upload your keystrokes, glide paths or messages to a correction service. No HBoard account, advertising SDK or analytics service is used.

Typing and local learning

The keyboard processes the text and surrounding context that the receiving application and HarmonyOS make available, to suggest words, correct spelling and edit text. The receiving application receives text that you enter and handles it under its own policy. HBoard respects the input restrictions reported by the platform; it does not bypass restricted text access.

When learning is enabled, personal vocabulary, word preferences, local contextual counts, optional correction feedback and touch/glide adaptation can be stored in HBoard’s application storage. Selecting a spelling in the suggestion bar can teach it. Learning from undone corrections is off by default; undoing a correction does not add the restored misspelling to your vocabulary. Enabling optional Undo feedback remembers rejected correction pairs locally.

Incognito pauses learning. Protected fields such as passwords and one-time codes disable relevant predictions and learning when identified by the platform. Word learning and Adaptive touch have separate controls. Turning either off pauses its new learning; it does not erase information already saved.

Information you choose to save

Settings, personal dictionary entries, text shortcuts, saved email addresses and permitted clipboard-history items are stored locally. Clipboard previews and history depend on your settings and HarmonyOS authorisation. HBoard filters sensitive clipboard items and suppresses collection in Incognito and protected fields. Pinned items can be retained until you remove them; other items follow your retention settings.

The optional Glide practice & calibration page records the displayed practice word, finger coordinates and keyboard geometry only after you tap Start recording. It stops when you stop or leave that page. Those practice recordings remain local and can be removed with Delete recordings. Normal glide recognition does not save a recording of your messages or upload finger paths.

Voice, images and translation

Voice typing uses the microphone only when you activate it and grant permission. Bundled Whisper models process audio on the device; Chinese voice input can use the HarmonyOS offline speech service. Audio and recognised text are not uploaded by HBoard to a speech server. Temporary audio buffers are used for recognition.

If you choose Scan text, the system camera or photo picker gives HBoard access to the image you select. Text recognition runs on the device. You decide whether to copy the result. HBoard does not upload selected images for OCR.

English/Chinese translation can use an offline model already installed on your device. This version does not download translation models. Voice input, OCR and translation do not upload audio, images or text to an HBoard service.

Optional dictionary downloads

Normal typing, correction, predictions and glide recognition work offline. Opening Languages or a language’s options does not start a network request. Only when you tap Dictionary Download, HBoard contacts GitHub’s API and raw-file delivery services for the requested language’s metadata, licence records and word list from GBTechReviews/harmonikeyboard-dicts. There are no automatic dictionary checks or background updates.

GitHub receives normal connection information such as your IP address and the language-file paths requested. HBoard does not send your typing, messages, personal words, touch data or learned history. Verified vocabulary is stored locally, works offline and supplements the included dictionary. Dictionary credits and licence notices are available in Languages. Restore bundled removes downloaded dictionaries without deleting your personal words.

Permissions and external services

  • Microphone: voice typing while in use.
  • Clipboard access: authorised clipboard previews, history and paste features.
  • Internet: user-requested dictionary checks and downloads only. Opening an external policy, repository or support link uses the external app you choose and its own network connection.
  • Device gesture awareness: optional automatic hand detection for layout adjustment.
  • Vibration: optional key and control feedback.

You can manage permissions in HarmonyOS settings. Camera/photo access is mediated by system pickers. HBoard does not request access to your contacts or location. GitHub also hosts this policy and handles its network services under GitHub’s privacy statement.

Retention, deletion and your choices

  • In Dictionary, remove individual learned words with Forget or use Delete learned words.
  • In Corrections and suggestions, Reset undone corrections removes saved correction rejection preferences. Reset touch learning clears touch adaptation.
  • Delete shortcuts, saved addresses and clipboard items in their respective settings. Delete calibration recordings on the practice page.
  • Exports and backups are created only when you request them. Choose their destination carefully: a backup can contain personal words and other saved keyboard information. HBoard cannot remove copies you export to other apps or storage.
  • Removing HBoard’s application data or uninstalling it removes its local application storage, subject to any backups or copies managed separately by HarmonyOS or by you.

HBoard does not operate a remote personal-data account or server-side typing history. We cannot retrieve a local dictionary from your device. You can contact the publisher about privacy or to request assistance with access, correction or deletion. If you email support, your email address and the information you include are received and used to handle that request; please avoid sending passwords or sensitive typing samples.

Security and other applications

HBoard uses HarmonyOS application storage and platform permission controls. Device backups, cloud synchronisation selected by you, the operating system and destination applications are governed by their respective settings and policies. Keep your device protected and review exported files before sharing them.

Your choices and support correspondence

You can inspect, correct or remove saved words and other local data using HBoard’s settings. The publisher does not have a remote copy of device-only keyboard data. If you email support, we use your address and message to respond and manage your request, based on our legitimate interest in providing support. Correspondence is retained while needed for the request and related follow-up; contact us to ask for access, correction or deletion. Where applicable, you may also request restriction, object to processing or request portability. You may complain to your local data-protection authority; UK users can contact the Information Commissioner’s Office.

Changes and contact

Updates will be published at this address with a revised date. For privacy questions, contact huaweiproreview@gmail.com. This policy covers HBoard for HarmonyOS; separately published HKeyboard or Android products have their own policies.
